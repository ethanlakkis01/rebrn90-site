Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

REBRN 90 ("we", "us") is an app run by Ethan Lakkis in Melbourne, Australia. This policy explains what information the REBRN 90 app and website collect, why, who it's shared with, and your choices. We handle personal information under the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

The short version: we collect what we need to run your plan, we never sell your data or show you ads, your Apple Health data is never used for advertising, and you can delete everything from inside the app.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and the first name you choose to show friends. You can use the app without an account; then your data stays only on your phone.
  • Your profile: the answers you give when setting up, such as age, sex, height, weight, goal, training experience and the days you can train. We use these to work out your training plan and calorie and protein targets.
  • What you log: weigh-ins, workouts (exercises, sets, reps and weights), meals you plan or eat, recipes you create, weekly check-ins (energy, hunger, sleep and training ratings out of 10, and any note), and the week reviews made from them.
  • Coach messages: what you write to the coach and its replies.
  • Apple Health data (only if you allow it): your step count and body weight. You choose this in Apple's Health permission screen and can turn it off at any time in the Health app.
  • Friends: your friend code, who you've added, and the effort numbers friends can see (days ticked, sessions this week and steps today).
  • Purchases: whether your subscription is active. Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your card details.
  • Technical information: basic logs our service providers keep to run and secure the service, such as when you signed in.

Some of this, like body weight, eating and health ratings, is sensitive information under Australian privacy law. We only collect it with your consent, which you give by entering it or allowing Apple Health, and only use it to provide the app to you.

2. How we use it

  • To build and adjust your training plan, meal plan and targets.
  • To back up your data and restore it if you change phones (when you're signed in).
  • To answer your coach messages.
  • To show your effort numbers to friends you've accepted.
  • To send sign-in codes, and to reply when you contact us.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We don't sell your information, use it for advertising, track you across other apps or websites, or use Apple Health data for marketing.

3. What friends can see

Only your chosen first name, the days you've ticked in your 90 days, your sessions this week and your steps today, and only for friends you've both accepted. Friends never see your weight, food, check-ins, coach messages or email address.

4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run REBRN 90. They process information for us under contract and only to provide their service:

  • Supabase: stores your account and backed-up data. Our database is hosted in Sydney, Australia.
  • Resend: sends sign-in emails, so it receives your email address.
  • Anthropic: provides the AI that writes coach replies. When you message the coach, your message, recent coach conversation and a summary of your plan (such as targets and your last check-in) are sent to generate the reply. Anthropic doesn't use this to train its models under its commercial terms.
  • Apple: handles App Store payments and, if you allow it, Apple Health on your phone.

Resend and Anthropic are based in the United States, so information they process may be handled outside Australia. We choose providers with strong security and privacy commitments. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

5. How we protect it

Information is encrypted in transit. Each account's data in our database is locked so only that account can read it. Your sign-in is stored in your iPhone's secure Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your information safe and will tell you and the regulator if a serious breach happens, as the law requires.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Account → Delete account), which permanently deletes your account and everything saved to it from our database. You can also email us to ask. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you delete the app.

7. Your choices and rights

You can see and edit most of your information in the app. You can ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing hello@rebrn90.app. If you have a privacy concern, contact us first. If you're not happy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

8. Age

REBRN 90 is for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

9. Not medical advice

REBRN 90 gives general fitness and nutrition guidance, not medical advice. See our Terms.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
