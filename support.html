Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → REBRN 90 → Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Apple processes refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, tap the person icon on Home, then Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything saved to it. You can also email us to ask.

My steps aren't showing

Open the Health app, tap your picture (top right), then Apps → REBRN90, and turn on Steps and Weight.

I didn't get my sign-in code

Codes come from hello@rebrn90.app and can take a minute. Check your junk folder, or tap "Send again" in the app.

Need more help with food or your health?

REBRN 90 isn't medical advice. If food or exercise feels hard to manage, talk to your GP, or contact the Butterfly Foundation on 1800 33 4673. In a crisis, call Lifeline on 13 11 14 or 000 in an emergency.
